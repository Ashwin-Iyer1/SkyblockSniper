Visible limitations.

Name, rarity and a hash of normalized lore form the display fingerprint. Color codes and extra whitespace are removed; pet levels, reforges, stars and lore differences remain distinct. This does not decode NBT and cannot guarantee that every hidden item attribute matches.

Only active, unclaimed BIN records qualify. Missing pages, conflicting UUIDs or mixed snapshot timestamps stop analysis. At least two other comparable listings are required. Single-listing groups, expired records and claimed auctions are counted in the audit below.

The bundled data is entirely synthetic. No real auction, purchase, game command, notification or clipboard action is involved.
